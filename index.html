      How to play

      1. It's the last quarter and you're behind. Each day you play a different club at their home ground. The higher they finished on the 2026 ladder, the bigger the gap.
      2. Seven prompts, seven kicks, the same for everyone. Type one answer before the 30-second shot clock runs out.
      3. One set shot a game. Tap the shot clock and it stops for the kick you're on. Take your time, but you only get one.
      4. Every answer is a kick from the centre bounce. Obvious answers barely go 15. Rare ones go long. Wrong answers go out on the full.
      5. Reach the goal line for a goal (6 points), then it's back to the centre. Four drop punts make two goals. Get in front before the siren.
      6. Each prompt hides one gem. Find it and it's a goal after the siren, from anywhere.
      7. Mind the post. Some prompts have one "clever" answer that everyone reaches for. It gets there from anywhere too, but it hits the post: one behind (1 point), and back to the centre.
      8. Nicknames and surnames are fine and small typos are forgiven. If a surname fits two people, you'll be asked which one and the clock stops.
      9. One go per round, no do-overs. Your results and record are saved in this browser.
      10. Then come the finals. After Round 23, four finals. Win all four and the flag is yours. Twelve wins in the season earns the double chance: lose the first final and you're still alive. A final can't be drawn: level after seven kicks means extra time, three more kicks from the centre, and the next score wins.
      11. Your 5th game, your 10th, and every 10th after gets a banner to run through.
      12. In the menu: pick your club and your side wears its colours. There's also sound, a calmer camera (Motion), and on phones you can swap the game's keys for your phone's own keyboard (Keys). Rain and wind are only for looks.

        Bontillion counts plays and answers to see which questions work. The counts are anonymous: no cookies, no accounts, nothing that says who you are.
