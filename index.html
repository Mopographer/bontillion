      How to play

      1. It's the last quarter and you're behind. Each day you play a different club. The higher they finished on the 2026 ladder, the bigger the gap.
      2. Seven prompts, seven kicks, the same for everyone. Type one answer before the 30-second shot clock runs out.
      3. Every answer is a kick from the centre bounce. Obvious answers barely go 15. Rare ones go long. Wrong answers go out on the full.
      4. Reach the goal line for a goal (6 points), then it's back to the centre. Four drop punts make two goals. Get in front before the siren.
      5. Each prompt hides one gem. Find it and it's a goal after the siren, from anywhere.
      6. Nicknames and surnames are fine and small typos are forgiven. If a surname fits two people, you'll be asked which one and the clock stops.
      7. One go per round, no do-overs. Your results and record are saved in this browser.
      8. In the menu: sound, a calmer camera (Motion), and on phones you can swap the game's keys for your phone's own keyboard (Keys).
